How to Deposit via Bank Transfer

1
Open your bank app, USSD (*966#, *737#, etc.) or internet banking.
2
Choose Transfer / Send Money select the bank shown above as destination bank.
3
Enter the account number —. The name — will appear — confirm it matches.
4
Enter your desired amount plus the flat fee (see fee table above). Example: want ₦1,000 credited send ₦1,050 (₦50 fee).
5
Complete the transfer. Return here and tap Refresh Balance — your wallet updates within seconds.
6
This account is permanent — save it in your bank app's favourites and use it any time.
Only send Nigerian Naira (NGN). Do not send from a foreign account or crypto wallet.

1

5

6

Payment links expire after for 30 minutes from when you tap Continue. If yours expires, tap Continue again to get a fresh one.
